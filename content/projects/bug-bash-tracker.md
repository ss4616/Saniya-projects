Turned an informal Friday ritual into a repeatable process: a spreadsheet-
backed tool that randomly assigns coverage areas to testers, logs each
finding straight into JIRA via its API, and produces a per-sprint defect
trend chart the team reviews in retro. Adopted by two other squads after the
first quarter.
