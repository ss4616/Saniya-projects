---
title: "Onboarding Charter Toolkit"
description: "A reusable project-charter template and stakeholder-mapping workbook that shortened new-initiative kickoffs from two weeks of back-and-forth to a single working session."
tags: ["Project Coordination", "Notion", "Risk Register"]
---

Standardized how new initiatives get scoped: one Notion template covering
objectives, in/out-of-scope boundaries, a stakeholder map, and a living risk
register. Rolled out across three product squads; kickoff-to-approval time
dropped from an average of two weeks of scattered emails to one 90-minute
working session.
